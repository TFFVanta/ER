# Logo System

## Primary Marks

### EXOTIC
EXOTIC is the main platform identity. It should present as the most controlled and product-grade mark in the system: sharp, balanced, minimal, and suited to advanced workspace software.

### MINGO
Use the MINGO wordmark as the authorship signature. It should feel quiet, premium, and exact rather than loud or illustrative.

### ER
Use the ER mark as the memorable emblem. It should anchor the identity system the way a strong symbol does for major technology products: recognizable at a glance, clean in monochrome, and consistent across product surfaces.

## Usage Rules

1. Lead with EXOTIC on product surfaces.
2. Use MINGO as signature, not as the primary product shell.
3. Use ER as the emblematic anchor and launch mark.
4. Keep all marks black and white by default.
5. Use accent color around the mark, not inside it, unless a defined asset explicitly requires otherwise.
6. Avoid cute, noisy, or novelty treatments.
7. Every mark must survive small-size usage, dark surfaces, light surfaces, and sharp UI contexts.

## Product Surface Rules

- Prefer black backgrounds, white typography, and gray structural layers for workspace UI.
- Use white backgrounds only when the product surface specifically needs a lighter working canvas.
- Favor sharp geometry, restrained radii, and strong alignment.
- Treat the mark like a precision brand asset, not decoration.

## Brand Formula
EXOTIC = system.
MINGO = signature.
ER = emblem.
Everything Is Exotic.
