# Logo System

## Primary Marks

### MINGO
Use the white outlined MINGO wordmark as the founder signature and clean identity stamp. It should feel simple, spaced, confident, and easy to recognize on black or white.

### ER
Use the bold ER bubble mark as the Exotic Remedy community/sticker identity. It should feel loud, memorable, streetwear-ready, playful, and collectible.

### EXOTIC
EXOTIC is the engineering platform. It should not visually fight MINGO or ER. It should use clean UI, cards, grids, black outlines, and pastel accent marks.

## Usage Rules
1. MINGO signs the work.
2. ER gives the brand personality.
3. EXOTIC organizes the technology.
4. Keep all marks black and white first.
5. Add pink, yellow, blue, or green only as supporting accents.
6. Never overcomplicate the logo. Recognition beats decoration.

## Brand Formula
MINGO = signature.
ER = culture.
EXOTIC = platform.
Everything Is Exotic.
