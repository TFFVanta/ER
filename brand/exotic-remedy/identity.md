# Exotic Remedy Brand Identity

Exotic Remedy is a separate venture from the EXOTIC AI platform (`brand/identity.md`) and does
not follow that system's black-and-white-only, operator-grade design law. It has its own
character: warm, mascot-led, approachable — built around a flamingo.

## Canonical direction

Chosen from the candidate assets in `brand/exotic-remedy/assets/`: **flat pastel color, with
black/white structural line work, in a classic (not trend-chasing) pattern language.**

Reference assets for this direction:
- `flamingo-mascot-color.png`, `flamingo-mascot-scene-color.png` — the mascot, flat pastel fill
  over bold black outline
- `flamingo-mascot-lineart.png` — the same mascot as pure black/white line art (structural base,
  usable anywhere color isn't available: stamps, single-color print, favicons)
- `er-mark-3d-blocks-bw.png`, `exotic-remedy-globe-emblem-bw.webp` — black/white structural marks
- `mingo-wordmark-thin-sans.png` — the wordmark register that fits "classic" (not the bubble
  wordmark, which is excluded from this direction)

**Explicitly excluded** from the canonical direction (kept in `assets/` for reference only, not
brand-approved): the bubble/sticker typography (`mingo-wordmark-bubble.png`,
`exotic-remedy-badge-bubble.png`, `er-mark-checkerboard-bubble.png`), the dotted/glitch poster
(`exotic-remedy-poster-glitch.png`), and the TV-static treatment (`er-mark-glitch-static.png`).

## Brand character

- Warm and approachable, not corporate — a mascot brand, not a logotype-first brand
- Classic, not trendy — the line work and pattern language should hold up over years, not read
  as a current meme aesthetic
- Structure comes from black/white line art; color is the flat pastel fill inside it, never the
  other way around
- Confident but soft — bold outlines, gentle interior color

## Marks

- **Mascot** (the flamingo) — primary brand carrier. Use the flat pastel version wherever color
  reproduction is available; drop to the pure line-art version for single-color contexts.
- **ER emblem** — secondary, structural black/white mark for compact spaces (favicons, stamps,
  small packaging).
- **Wordmark** — the thin/classic register only. The bubble register is not part of this
  direction.

## Design law

1. Black/white line work is the skeleton; flat pastel color is the fill — never invert that.
2. The mascot leads; the wordmark and emblem support.
3. Classic over trendy — no glitch, no dot-pattern noise, no sticker-pack bubble type.
4. Keep the outline weight bold and consistent across every mark (mascot, emblem, wordmark).
5. White background is the default stage.
