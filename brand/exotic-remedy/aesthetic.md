# Exotic Remedy Aesthetic

Concrete visual-system rules underneath `identity.md`'s brand law. Where identity.md says *what*
the direction is, this says *how* to draw it.

## Line work

Observed in `flamingo-mascot-lineart.png`, the structural reference:
- **Single, bold, uniform stroke weight** — no thin/thick contrast within one outline.
- **Rounded stroke ends and joins**, not sharp miters — reads soft, not technical/blueprint.
- **Loose, confident, slightly hand-drawn** — small imperfections in curve are fine and
  desirable; do not over-vectorize into perfectly mechanical curves. This is what separates the
  direction from a corporate/geometric logo system.
- Interior detail (feather lines, eye, beak) uses the same weight as the silhouette outline, not
  a lighter secondary weight.

## Color application

- Fill is always flat and solid — no gradients, no shading, no drop shadows on the mascot itself.
- One fill color per shape region (body = `remedy-pink`, beak = `remedy-gold`); color never
  crosses a black outline boundary.
- Outline is always `remedy-ink` (pure black), regardless of what color it encloses.
- Never fill the background — `remedy-paper` (white) stays empty behind the mark. The mascot
  should be usable as a sticker/die-cut, which requires this.

## Pattern system

"Classic patterns" means traditional textile/print motifs, used sparingly as background texture
— never as the mark's own outline treatment (that's what `exotic-remedy-poster-glitch.png` and
`er-mark-checkerboard-bubble.png` got wrong, and why they're excluded).

Approved motifs, always rendered in `remedy-ink` on `remedy-paper` (two-color only, no tints):
- **Pinstripe** — thin, evenly spaced parallel lines
- **Polka dot** — evenly spaced, evenly sized dots
- **Gingham/houndstooth** — small-scale woven-look check, not the large flat checkerboard seen in
  `er-mark-checkerboard-bubble.png` (that reads as a sticker-pack prop, not a classic textile)

Rules:
- Pattern is a **background or accent element only** — packaging border, card backing, textile —
  never behind or replacing the mascot's own line work in the same frame.
- Pattern scale must stay small relative to the mascot so it reads as texture, not as competing
  imagery.
- No more than one pattern per composition.

## Typography

- Wordmark: the thin/classic geometric register (`mingo-wordmark-thin-sans.png`) — evenly
  weighted, generous letterspacing, all-caps.
- Supporting/body text: a plain humanist sans (system default is fine — this isn't a typography-
  led brand). Weight should stay light-to-regular; avoid bold body copy competing with the
  wordmark.
- Never set body or supporting text in the bubble/sticker letterforms — those are excluded from
  this direction entirely, not just for the wordmark.

## Mascot posing

Based on the two approved poses (`flamingo-mascot-color.png`, `flamingo-mascot-scene-color.png`):
- Wings extended, one-leg-forward stance reads as the default/neutral pose — calm, open, a
  greeting posture, not mid-action.
- Expression stays simple: two small dot/oval eyes, no eyebrows or mouth beyond the beak shape.
  Avoid pushing toward exaggerated cartoon emotion (that drifts toward the excluded
  `flamingo-mascot-scene-color.png` zoo-scene busyness — fine as a one-off illustration, not as
  the reusable brand mark).
- The mascot stands alone on white in brand contexts. Scene backgrounds (buildings, other
  animals) are illustration-only use, not part of the core mark system.

## Layout & spacing

- Generous whitespace around the mascot — it should never crowd the frame edge.
- When paired with the wordmark, the mascot leads (larger, positioned first/above); the wordmark
  is secondary and smaller.
- The ER emblem is for contexts too small for the full mascot (favicons, stamps) — never use both
  the emblem and the full mascot in the same lockup.

## Quick reference: do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Flat pastel fill inside bold black outline | Gradients, shadows, or 3D bevel on the mascot |
| Loose, slightly hand-drawn line quality | Perfectly mechanical/geometric vector curves |
| One small classic pattern as background texture | Pattern behind or replacing the mark's line work |
| Thin/classic wordmark register | Bubble/sticker lettering |
| Mascot alone on white | Busy scene backgrounds in the core mark |
